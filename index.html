VibrateAuto — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

VibrateAuto automatically switches your phone to vibrate when you arrive at a synagogue or another place you have saved, and restores your ringer when you leave. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your information.

VibrateAuto has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. There is no server that belongs to us, and we do not receive, store, or have access to any of your data.

Information the app uses

InformationWhy
Approximate and precise location To detect when you arrive at and leave a saved place, including in the background while the app is closed.
Addresses you type into search To look up a place when you create a rule.

Where information is sent

Most of the app works entirely on your device. Information leaves your phone only in these three cases:

Sent toWhat is sentWhy
godaven.com Your approximate coordinates, the current time and day of the week To retrieve a list of nearby synagogues. GoDaven is an independent service with its own privacy practices.
Google Maps Platform Text you type into address search, and your location while a map is displayed To suggest addresses and to draw the map.
Google (Android system) Coordinates of a point you tap on the map To convert that point into a street address, using Android's built-in service.

Your location is not sent anywhere while the app is simply running in the background watching for arrivals and departures. That work is done on the device by Android itself.

Information kept on your device only

  • The rules you create, including their names, addresses and coordinates
  • Which nearby synagogues you have switched off
  • Your settings, such as the rule radius
  • A local diagnostic log (vibrateauto.log) recording app errors

None of this is transmitted to us or to anyone else. If Android Backup is switched on for your device, Android may include these files in your own Google account backup; that backup is controlled by you and by Google, not by us.

Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionWhy
Location, including in the background The core function: detecting arrival and departure while your phone is in your pocket.
Notifications To show an ongoing notice while a rule is holding your ringer, so you always know the app is active and can undo it.
Do Not Disturb access Only required for rules set to full silent. Vibrate rules work without it.
Alarms and reminders Only required for rules that switch at a set time.
Ignore battery optimisation Android otherwise stops the app after a few days, and rules would silently stop working.

Retention and deletion

All app data is stored on your device. Uninstalling VibrateAuto removes it. You can also clear it at any time from Android Settings, under Apps → VibrateAuto → Storage. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Children

VibrateAuto is not directed at children and does not collect information from anyone.
